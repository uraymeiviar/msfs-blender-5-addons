### Trigger

While the specification was drafted (`docs/spec/`, not yet committed), the maintainer asked how far zoning is built. Zone empties, the zone rows and warnings, and the zone density factor work (spec ZON-001 to ZON-004); the zone tool (materials per zone and class, UV decisions 22 to 32, spec ZON-010, ZON-011) is not built. The maintainer then widened the goal from zoning to material management.

### The Maintainer's Objectives

Blender's material information is scattered; the artist should get immediate information about:

1. materials that are never used (warning);
2. materials without an MSFS material assigned (warning);
3. materials shared across zones;
4. materials whose objects should not be merged;
5. MSFS materials that make no sense without a texture;
6. MSFS materials whose parameters make no sense or could lead to an error.

And should see, per zone, the distribution of materials: how many objects use each, and how much island surface each needs; with a tool that recommends a texture size from the density requirement (a set used by few objects may need only 256 × 256 if packing can hold it at its density; a set used by many objects may not fit even at 8K and needs a second material). Texture imbalance is a further objective: a material with albedo, ORM and emissive maps where only a small part emits light leaves most of the emissive map black while albedo and ORM are full; such a material is better split in two.

### Findings Given in the Discussion

- **Transparency sorting** (MSFS Authoring Guide `how-msfs-renders.md`, `materials.md`, citing the SDK): transparent surfaces (glass, windshield, decals) are drawn back to front, sorted by the centre of each mesh's bounding box, not per triangle. Draw Order (-999 to 999) corrects the order for decal, glass and windshield materials only. Today a merge group puts all its transparent parts into one `<name>_TRANSPARENT` mesh, so two gauge glasses in one group can no longer be sorted against each other.
- **Dispersed decals** (maintainer): decals render last onto the surface behind them. With surface (a), decal (a), surface (b), decal (b) one behind the other, a merged decal mesh can draw decal (a) onto surface (b) at some view angles.
- **Recommended size exists in part:** Check All already reports per texture set whether the islands fit at their density, the smaller size that would do, the needed size, or "does not fit even at 8192" (spec UV-PCK-005, UV-PCK-008).
- **Emissive coverage before texturing** cannot be read from the geometry: no face says it will glow. The tool can know it only when the artist marks the emissive parts (or, after texturing, by measuring the emissive image).
- **Checks 2, 5 and 6 are MSFS-specific** by nature, against the preference for model-agnostic tools (UV decision 100).

### Maintainer Decisions

1. **Material management gets its own panel, Materials,** in the FSS Tools sidebar after Zones & Merge Tree. Zoning is one part of it, not its centre.
2. **No strict naming rule for material classes.** Materials are not classified by a name pattern such as `<SCENE>_<ZONE>_<CLASS>`; the artist names materials freely.
3. **Material kinds are detected from the material, not tagged:** emissive when the material's emissive slot has a texture assigned; transparent when its alpha mode is Blend (decision 9); decal from the MSFS material type, which is explicit.
4. **Objects the model XML refers to are tagged by the artist, with the tag switched to Don't Merge** (the existing tag switch, spec TAG-005). The merge still does not read the model XML (tags-and-merge decision S1.7).
5. **The artist guide explains** texture imbalance and the problems of merging transparent surfaces and dispersed decals.
6. **The zone tool (UV decisions 22 to 32, spec ZON-010, ZON-011) is on hold:** not built, not cancelled, to be decided later.
7. **The sidebar is split into two tabs, FSS Tools and FSS MSFS Tools,** in the UI only (one add-on): tools that work on any model in FSS Tools (Zones & Merge Tree, Tags, UV Tools, Utilities), MSFS-specific tools in FSS MSFS Tools (LOD, Materials). Auto Seam's and Visibility Scoring's material-class exceptions, the merge and Scan Names stay in FSS Tools. (The label was first proposed as FSS MSFS; the maintainer chose FSS MSFS Tools while Session 3 ran.)
8. **No "keep separate" switch on materials.** Objects that must not merge (colliders, clickspots, screens, glass that overlaps glass) are kept out by tags: the artist selects the objects that use a material (for example the collider material), gives them a tag, and switches the tag to Don't Merge. The merge decides nothing from material kinds.
9. **Transparent means MSFS alpha mode Blend,** not an alpha value below 1 (alpha can come from a texture).
10. **Texture imbalance is detected only after the emissive texture is painted,** by measuring the emissive image under each object's islands; there is no earlier way. A material without an emissive texture has no emissive imbalance, even when its objects glow.
11. **The Materials list marks each material's kind on its row:** transparent (MSFS alpha mode Blend) and emissive (an emissive texture assigned), so the artist tells them apart at a glance. The detection is decision 3. The whole Materials panel, marks and later MSFS checks included, is in the FSS MSFS Tools tab (decision 7), not split across tabs.

### Proposed Content of the Materials Panel (Phase 1)

Shown to the maintainer and accepted with decision 11 added; to become the phase 1 hand-off. Read-only, with a Refresh button (counting users and areas on every redraw is too slow, and panels must not write while drawing):

- **Material list** (scrolling UIList, name filter, All / Warnings): per material the warning sign (never used, shared across zones), the kind marks (decision 11), the number of objects with faces using it (an empty slot does not count), the zones of those objects ("–" without a zone), the texture size and the last Pack or Check All result.
- **Active material box:** objects (total, hidden, shared meshes); per zone the objects and 3D area, with the warning when more than one zone; the total 3D area and the square side it needs at its density, labelled as an estimate (area × density² only, no packing); texture size and last run; Select Objects (visible objects using it, for tagging, decision 8) and Select in <zone> per zone.
- **Check All:** the Packing operator, also here.
- **By Zone** (collapsed subpanel): per zone its materials with objects, 3D area and share of the zone's surface; objects outside every zone under "(no zone)".
- Not in phase 1: MSFS checks 2, 5, 6 (phase 2, in the same panel), split helpers and imbalance (phase 3), any action that changes materials.

### Definitions

- **Material in scope:** every material in `bpy.data.materials` except grease pencil materials (`material.is_grease_pencil`).
- **Used by an object:** a mesh object of the view layer (visible or hidden) has at least one face whose material index points at a slot with this material. A material only in an empty slot (no faces), only on objects outside the view layer, or in no slot is **never used**.
- **Zones of a material:** the nearest `@ZONE_` at or above each using object (`merge.nearest_zone`), shown without the marker; objects outside every zone count as **(no zone)**. **Shared across zones:** used by objects in more than one zone; (no zone) counts as a zone here.
- **Kinds (decisions 3, 9, 11):** transparent when `material.msfs_alpha_mode == 'BLEND'`; emissive when `material.msfs_emissive_texture` is not None. Read with `getattr(..., None)` so the panel works without the exporter add-on.
- **Area:** world-space 3D area in m² of the faces using the material (`uv_pack.face_areas_cm2`). A mesh shared by several objects counts once (its UVs are shared), measured on its first using object; objects are counted each.
- **Estimate:** the side in pixels of a square that holds the material's faces at the top of their density range without visibility: `sqrt(sum over faces of area_cm2 x (target_density x factor_high)^2)`, where `factor_high` is the high end of `uv_marks.density(obj)` for the face's object. Shown as "about N px square (estimate)". No packing, margins or visibility; the packed truth is Check All's result.

## Session 17: Draft of the MSFS Material Check Rules (Phase 2, MTL-020)

- **Date:** 20261029
- **Developer:** uraymeiviar
- **Agent:** Claude Code (Opus 5.5); research by a sub-agent
- **Branch:** main
- **Base Commit SHA:** 14604c5

Proposals only; nothing below is decided. The reviewer checked five of the sources against the SDK pages and the exporter code: the unset type value `NONE` ("Disabled"), Draw Order "effective for Decal, Glass and Windshield materials", texture sides a multiple of 4 and at least 8, the "$" prefix acting as a collision material, and a decal with every channel off being invisible.

Draft for review rule by rule. Nothing here is decided. Families are the maintainer's objectives of the
material-management journal, Session 1:

- (2) material without an MSFS material (warning);
- (5) MSFS material that makes no sense without a texture (informational before texturing, per MTL-020);
- (6) MSFS material whose parameters make no sense or could lead to an error.

Sources: the exporter in this repository (`io_scene_gltf2_msfs_fss`), the MSFS 2024 SDK pages under
`F:\new-fss-aircraft-ejet\doc\msfs-sdk\pages\` (SDK 1.7.3; there are no MSFS 2020 SDK pages locally), and the FSS
authoring guide `F:\new-fss-aircraft-ejet\doc\msfs-authoring\`. Paths below are relative to `msfs-sdk\pages\`
(SDK) or to the repository root (exporter code).

Exporter behaviour was established by reading the code, not by running an export. Where a rule depends on it, it
says "code reading".

### Common Definitions

- **Used material:** as in the Materials panel (phase 1): a mesh object of the view layer has a face whose
  material index points at a slot holding the material. Rules apply to used materials only. A material that is
  never used already gets the phase 1 warning.
- **Unset MSFS material:** `material.msfs_material_type == 'NONE'` (shown as "Disabled"; the enum default). A
  material created without the exporter add-on reads the default as well, so it counts as unset. There is no
  separate "Standard by default" state: Standard is `'msfs_standard'` and must be chosen.
- **Type groups** (values of `msfs_material_type`):
  - DECALS = `msfs_decal`, `msfs_geo_decal_frosted`, `msfs_geo_decal_blendmasked`
  - GLASSY = `msfs_glass`, `msfs_windshield`
  - ALPHA_CHOICE = types whose panel lets the artist pick the alpha mode: `msfs_standard`, `msfs_clearcoat`,
    `msfs_anisotropic`, `msfs_hair`, `msfs_sss`, `msfs_sail` (and `msfs_fresnel_fade`, `msfs_tree`,
    `msfs_vegetation`, left out on purpose: see the rules). Glass, windshield, decals, ghost and environment
    occluder are set to BLEND by their builder and do not show the alpha mode (exporter code
    `blender/material/msfs_material_*.py`).
- **Slot lists of a type:** `MSFS2024_MaterialPropUpdate.get_material(material).attributes` (each builder class in
  `blender/material/` lists the properties its panel shows); `None` for `'NONE'`.
- **Has alpha channel:** for a PNG, read the IHDR colour type from the file (byte 25: 4 or 6 = with alpha); a JPG has
  none. Blender's `image.depth` (32 or 64 / 128 for RGBA) is a fallback only; it was not checked here that it
  reflects the file's channels for every format. If the file cannot be read, the rule says nothing.
- **Black:** every component of `msfs_emissive_factor` is 0.0.
- **Animated:** a property is animated when `material.animation_data` has an F-curve whose `data_path` is that
  property. Rules on values that can be animated skip animated properties (a value of 0 at rest may be switched
  on by the animation).

### Rules

#### R1: No MSFS material

- **Family / severity:** 2 / warning
- **Condition:** used material with `msfs_material_type == 'NONE'`.
- **Panel says:** "No MSFS material type: MSFS 2024 exports this material untextured, white and fully metallic."
- **Source:** SDK `models-and-textures/textures/materials/flightsim-materials.md`: "we recommend that you *only* use
  the ones listed on this page". Exporter code `io/exp/gltf_hooks.py:gather_material_hook` clears the textures the
  Khronos exporter found, then `io/com/extensions/material/asobo_material_common.py:AsoboMaterialCommon.to_extension`
  writes only the `msfs_*` values with no type check; for an unset material those are the defaults
  (`msfs_base_color_factor` white, `msfs_metallic_factor` 1.0, `msfs_roughness_factor` 1.0, no textures;
  `blender/utils/msfs_material_utils.py`).
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN (the SDK recommendation; the export result is from code reading).
- **MSFS 2020:** the MSFS 2020 exporter lets the Khronos exporter build the material from the node tree
  (`_msfs2020/io/msfs_khronos_patches.py` skips its fix for `'NONE'`), so the textures are exported but no MSFS
  extensions. Still a warning; the sentence would say "exported as a plain glTF material without MSFS settings".

#### R2: Decal without a base colour texture

- **Family / severity:** 5 / info (warning once the material has any other texture, i.e. texturing has started)
- **Condition:** `msfs_material_type == 'msfs_decal'` and `msfs_base_color_texture is None`.
- **Panel says:** "Decal without a Base Color texture: a decal needs at least its albedo texture."
- **Source:** SDK `models-and-textures/modeling/aircraft/airframe/airframe-details.md`: "The only texture that is
  needed for this material is the albedo".
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.

#### R3: Porthole without its albedo or emissive texture

- **Family / severity:** 5 / info (warning once another texture is assigned)
- **Condition:** `msfs_material_type == 'msfs_porthole'` and (`msfs_base_color_texture is None` or
  `msfs_emissive_texture is None`).
- **Panel says:** "Porthole needs a Base Color texture (frame and glass, depth in alpha) and an Emissive texture
  (the interior seen through the window)."
- **Source:** SDK `models-and-textures/modeling/aircraft/airframe/parallax-windows.md`: "For this material to work
  correctly you will need **four** textures"; the emissive texture "is the heart of the effect". The normal map is
  optional on that page, and the OMR is not special, so only albedo and emissive are required here.
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.

#### R4: Wiper lines without a wiper mask

- **Family / severity:** 5 / warning (the artist switched the effect on)
- **Condition:** `msfs_material_type == 'msfs_windshield'` and `msfs_windshield_wiper_lines` is True and
  `msfs_windshield_wiper_mask_texture is None`.
- **Panel says:** "Wiper Lines is on but no Wiper Mask texture is assigned; the effect needs one."
- **Source:** SDK `models-and-textures/textures/materials/flightsim-material-parameters.md` (Wiper Lines): "This
  effect requires a Wiper Mask (RGBA) texture".
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.
- **MSFS 2020:** the MSFS 2020 exporter has no wiper lines; MSFS 2024 only.

#### R5: Iridescence without a thickness texture

- **Family / severity:** 5 / warning
- **Condition:** `msfs_use_iridescent` is True and `msfs_iridescent_thickness_texture is None`.
- **Panel says:** "Use Iridescent is on but no Iridescent Thickness texture is assigned; the effect reads its
  thickness from that texture."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-material-parameters.md` (Use Iridescence): "will use the Iridescent
  Thickness (R) texture to create an iridescent effect". The exporter writes the extension whenever the switch is on,
  for any type (`asobo_material_iridescent.py:to_extension`).
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.
- **MSFS 2020:** MSFS 2024 only.

#### R6: Transparent alpha mode with nothing transparent

- **Family / severity:** 5 (no texture yet: info) / 6 (texture without alpha: warning)
- **Condition:** type in ALPHA_CHOICE, `msfs_alpha_mode in ('MASK', 'BLEND', 'DITHER')`, `msfs_base_color_factor[3]
  == 1.0`, and either `msfs_base_color_texture is None` (info) or the texture has no alpha channel (warning).
- **Panel says (info):** "Alpha mode is <mode> but alpha is 1 and no Base Color texture gives alpha yet: the material
  draws fully opaque." **(warning):** "Alpha mode is <mode> but the Base Color texture has no alpha channel and
  the base alpha is 1: nothing is transparent; use Opaque or add alpha to the texture."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-material-textures.md` (Base Color): "If the fourth component (A) is
  present ... Otherwise, an alpha of 1.0 is assumed."
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN (the effect); the alpha detection itself depends on reading the file (see Common
  Definitions).
- **Note:** `msfs_fresnel_fade` is left out (its opacity comes from the camera angle), as are tree and vegetation
  (R15).

#### R7: Glass or windshield drawn opaque

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** type in GLASSY, `msfs_base_color_factor[3] == 1.0` and `msfs_base_color_texture is None`.
- **Panel says:** "Glass with base alpha 1 and no Base Color texture: it will look opaque; the SDK setup uses an
  alpha of about 0.01."
- **Source:** SDK `models-and-textures/modeling/aircraft/cockpit/textures/translucent-elements.md`, glass setup step:
  "Set the alpha of the **Base Colour** to 0.01"; `.../airframe/windshield-and-windows.md` step 3 (alpha about
  0.01, or alpha 0 with an albedo texture giving the opacity).
- **Certainty:** JUDGEMENT. The SDK gives this as a setup step, not as a rule, and does not say what alpha 1 does
  in the glass shader; "looks opaque" is inferred from the BLEND mode the builder sets.

#### R8: Alpha value without effect

- **Family / severity:** 6 / info
- **Condition:** type in ALPHA_CHOICE and either (a) `msfs_alpha_mode == 'OPAQUE'` and `msfs_base_color_factor[3] <
  1.0`, or (b) `msfs_alpha_mode == 'MASK'` and `msfs_alpha_cutoff == 0.0`.
- **Panel says:** (a) "Base alpha is below 1 but the alpha mode is Opaque: the alpha is ignored." (b) "Mask with
  Alpha Cutoff 0: every pixel passes, so nothing is cut out."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-materials.md` (Alpha Mode, OPAQUE): "fully opaque and any alpha values are
  ignored"; (Alpha Cutoff): "If the alpha value is greater than or equal to the Alpha Cutoff value then it is
  rendered as fully opaque". (The MASK paragraph of the same page says the opposite, "above the alpha cutoff ...
  are discarded"; the Alpha Cutoff paragraph and the glTF definition agree with each other, so (b) follows them.)
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.

#### R9: Emissive texture or colour without effect

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** (`msfs_emissive_texture is not None` or `msfs_emissive_factor` is not black) and
  (`msfs_emissive_factor` is black or `msfs_emissive_scale == 0.0`), with neither `msfs_emissive_factor` nor
  `msfs_emissive_scale` animated. Types: every type except `msfs_invisible`, `msfs_environment_occluder`, and
  `msfs_porthole` / `msfs_parallax_window` (their emissive slot is the interior image).
- **Panel says:** "Emissive texture assigned but the Emissive Color is black (or the Emissive Scale is 0): the
  part will not glow."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-materials.md` (Emissive): "this colour is multiplied with the texel values
  of the texture". Exporter code `asobo_material_common.py:AsoboMaterialCommon.to_extension` writes
  `emissiveFactor = msfs_emissive_factor x msfs_emissive_scale`, so a black colour or a zero scale exports a zero
  factor. The default `msfs_emissive_factor` is black, so an artist who only assigns a texture hits this.
- **Certainty:** JUDGEMENT. The SDK contradicts itself: `.../airframe/exterior-lights.md` says that with an emissive
  map "the emissive value *will have no effect*" and that the colour comes from the Base Colour. Which one the sim
  follows was not tested; a test in the sim would settle it.
- **MSFS 2020:** the same condition; the MSFS 2020 export divides the scale by the emissive reference, which does
  not change a zero.

#### R10: Draw Order on a type that ignores it

- **Family / severity:** 6 / info
- **Condition:** `msfs_draw_order_offset != 0` and type not in DECALS or GLASSY.
- **Panel says:** "Draw Order only affects decal, glass and windshield materials; here it does nothing."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-materials.md` (Draw Order): "This parameter is effective for Decal, Glass
  and Windshield materials". FSS authoring guide `materials.md` (Transparent Materials) repeats it.
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN (counting the two geometry-decal types as decals is a reading of "Decal").

#### R11: Decal with every channel switched off

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** `msfs_material_type == 'msfs_decal'` and `msfs_scenery_channel`, `msfs_terrain_channel` and
  `msfs_simobject_channel` are all False.
- **Panel says:** "All decal channels are off: the decal will be invisible in the sim."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-material-parameters.md` (Decal Channel Masks): "if you *un-check* all
  options, **the decal will be invisible in the simulation**". The exporter writes the channels only in decal mode
  "default", i.e. for `msfs_decal` (`asobo_material_geometry_decal.py:to_extension`).
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.
- **MSFS 2020:** the MSFS 2020 exporter has no channel masks; MSFS 2024 only.

#### R12: Clearcoat texture ignored by Use Uniform Roughness

- **Family / severity:** 6 / info
- **Condition:** `msfs_material_type == 'msfs_clearcoat'`, `msfs_clearcoat_inverse_roughness` (panel: "Use Uniform
  Roughness") is True and `msfs_clearcoat_color_roughness_texture is not None`.
- **Panel says:** "Use Uniform Roughness is on, so the Clearcoat colour/roughness texture is not used."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-material-parameters.md` (Use Uniform Base Roughness): "When checked, it will
  disable the Clearcoat Colour (RGB) ClearCoat Roughness (A) texture slot".
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.

#### R13: Detail texture reused in another slot

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** an Image assigned to `msfs_detail_color_texture`, `msfs_detail_occlusion_metallic_roughness_texture`
  or `msfs_detail_normal_texture` of any used material is also assigned to any non-detail slot (base colour, OMR,
  normal, emissive, clearcoat, blend mask, ...) of any used material. Compare Image datablocks and, to be safe, the
  resolved file path.
- **Panel says:** "<image> is a detail map here and a regular texture in <material/slot>: the sim compresses detail
  maps differently, so the other use will look wrong."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-material-textures.md` (Detail Color, Detail ORM, Detail Normal): "these
  textures cannot be re-used in other texture slots".
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN for the standard detail slots. Open point: the windshield uses `msfs_detail_color_texture`
  for "Details 1 (R), Icing Mask (G), Details 2 (B)" and GeoDecalFrosted uses the detail ORM slot for the melt
  pattern; whether those are compressed the same way is not stated, so the rule treats the property, not the label.

#### R14: Material type not used by MSFS 2024

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** `msfs_material_type in ('msfs_tree', 'msfs_vegetation', 'msfs_tire')`.
- **Panel says:** "<type> is not used by MSFS 2024; use Standard (or the type the part needs)."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-materials.md` (Tree, Vegetation): "This material is not currently used in
  Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024"; `.../materials/flightsim-material-textures.md` (Tire): "This material is not
  currently used in the simulation."
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.
- **MSFS 2020:** these three, and also `msfs_geo_decal_blendmasked`, `msfs_sail` and `msfs_propeller`, do not exist in

- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.

#### R16: Texture left in a slot this type does not have

- **Family / severity:** 6 / info
- **Condition:** a texture property (any `msfs_*` Image pointer) is not None and is not in the slot list of the
  material's type (Common Definitions). Typical cases: an Invisible or Environment Occluder material with a base
  colour texture, a Standard material with a windshield texture left from an earlier type.
- **Panel says:** "<slot> holds a texture this material type does not show; it is still exported. Clear it or
  change the type."
- **Source:** exporter code: `asobo_material_common.py:AsoboMaterialCommon.to_extension` exports the four main

- **Certainty:** JUDGEMENT. That such a texture is exported is code reading; whether the sim reads it for that type,

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** `material.name.startswith('$')`.
- **Panel says:** "Names starting with $ make the material a collision material in MSFS 2024; rename it."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-materials.md` (Note On Referencing Materials): the prefix "forces the
  material to act like a collision material".
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.
- **MSFS 2020:** the SDK note is about MSFS 2024 (the prefix came from FSX); no local MSFS 2020 source.

#### R18: Texture size the sim does not accept

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** for each Image assigned to any `msfs_*` slot of a used material: `image.size[0] % 4 != 0` or
  `image.size[1] % 4 != 0` or either side `< 8`. Skip images whose size is 0 (not loaded; R20).
- **Panel says:** "<image> is <w> x <h>: textures must be multiples of 4 pixels and at least 8 x 8."
- **Source:** SDK `models-and-textures/textures/texturing-technical-information.md` (Quality): "input texture
  dimensions **must be a multiple of `4` pixels**". FSS authoring guide `texturing.md` (File Rules).
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN.

#### R19: Two different images with the same file name

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** two distinct image files (different resolved absolute paths) used by MSFS slots of used materials
  have the same file name (`os.path.basename`, case-insensitive).
- **Panel says:** "<name> is the file name of two different images (<folder A>, <folder B>); the sim finds textures
  by name only, so one will replace the other."
- **Source:** SDK `.../materials/flightsim-material-textures.md` (introduction): "Only the name of the texture is
  exported, the relative path is ignored."
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN (the consequence, one replacing the other, is direct from the name-only lookup).

#### R20: Image file missing

- **Family / severity:** 6 / warning
- **Condition:** an Image in an `msfs_*` slot of a used material has `image.source == 'FILE'`, no `packed_file`, and

- **Source:** FSS authoring guide `texturing.md` (File Rules): "An absolute path to another PC's drive shows as
  magenta in Blender and in the export. **[Verified]**"
- **Certainty:** CERTAIN (verified by FSS, not an SDK statement).

### Differences Between MSFS 2020 and MSFS 2024

- **Unset material (R1):** MSFS 2024 exports the `msfs_*` defaults without textures (white, metallic 1); MSFS 2020
  exports the node tree as a plain glTF material without MSFS extensions (code reading).
- **Types:** MSFS 2020 has 16 types; `msfs_geo_decal_blendmasked`, `msfs_sail`, `msfs_propeller`, `msfs_tree`,
  `msfs_vegetation` and `msfs_tire` are MSFS 2024 only and export as Standard for MSFS 2020
  (`_msfs2020/compat.py`). Tree, vegetation and tire are unused in MSFS 2024 too (R14).
- **Features only in the MSFS 2024 export:** wiper lines and wiper mask (R4), iridescence (R5), decal channel masks
  (R11), Flip Back Face Normal, rain options. The MSFS 2020 property set (`_msfs2020/com/msfs_material_props.py`)
  has none of them.
- **Emissive scale:** cd/m² for MSFS 2024 (default 1000), divided by the scene's MSFS 2020 emissive reference for
  MSFS 2020 (`_msfs2020/compat.py`). No rule depends on the size of the value, only on zero.
- **Draw Order:** the MSFS 2020 exporter skips it for invisible and environment occluder; the MSFS 2024 exporter
  writes it for every type. R10 holds for both.
- **SDK coverage:** every SDK citation is from the MSFS 2024 SDK 1.7.3. No MSFS 2020 SDK pages are available
  locally, so rules marked "same" for MSFS 2020 are assumed, not cited.
