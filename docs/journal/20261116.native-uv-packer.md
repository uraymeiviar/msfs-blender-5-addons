# Native UV Packer

Our own UV packer in C++, replacing Blender's Pack Islands in UV Auto Packing. The densities, the texture sets, reading
and writing the UVs stay in Python (`fss_blender_tools/uv_pack.py`); everything that arranges islands moves to C++.
Background: UV journal ([20261019.automated-uv-unwrap.md](20261019.automated-uv-unwrap.md)), Sessions 103 (the raster
placement `uv_fill`), 124 (repair), 125 (Keep Pinned Islands) and 126 (Target Area).

## Session 1: Research and Requirements

- **Date:** 20261116
- **Developer:** uraymeiviar
- **Agent:** Claude Code (Opus 5.5)

### Why

With Keep Pinned Islands and a Target Area, the maintainer saw islands scattered over the area. Measured on a copy of
the E2 cockpit (`MAT_LIT_COCKPIT_INTERACTION`, 688 islands, 130 pinned; area x 0.5, y 0, w 0.5, h 0.5, 35 pinned
islands inside it): Pack Islands does not see the pinned islands, the raster layout (`uv_fill.place`) is not taken
because the pinned islands make both layouts equally large, and the repair moves 392 islands one by one into whatever
free place is left. Preferring the raster layout when islands are kept moves none (0 gaps or overlaps beyond the 497 of
the pinned islands among themselves). No Upright island turned (largest deviation 0.011 degrees). The maintainer then
asked for a packer of our own: "can we create our own uv packer in c++, so we have full control, and its faster and
better than blender's".

### Research

How packers and nesting methods place islands (sources below):

| Method | Used by | Tightness | Speed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shelves of bounding boxes (fold and push) | Blender's Alpaca step for the small islands; Chameleon, FastAtlas, TABI (GPU atlases) | loose: boxes, not shapes | milliseconds |
| Raster ("Tetris"): islands as pixel masks on an occupancy grid, positions tried, the best kept | Levy et al. 2002, xatlas, Houdini UV Layout, Blender's exact-shape mode, likely UVPackmaster, our `uv_fill` | good, fills holes and cavities | seconds; positions x orientations |
| No-fit polygon with a genetic or greedy search | SVGnest, Deepnest | somewhat tighter | minutes |
| Overlap-tolerant local search (items may overlap, then are pushed apart while the strip shrinks) | sparrow on jagua-rs (2025, Rust) | best published nesting density (+1 to +4 points over earlier methods) | minutes (20 min default) |
| Learned gradient fields | GFPack++ (ICCV 2025) | +4 to +9 points over xatlas on UV atlas sets | seconds on a GPU; training; overlaps possible |

- **xatlas** (read in its source, `findChartLocation_bruteForce`): charts sorted by perimeter, largest first,
  conservatively rasterised and dilated by the padding; every position in two orientations (0 and 90 degrees) tried;
  score `extent^2 + area` of the atlas with the chart, ties to the one nearest the origin. Our `uv_fill.place` is the
  same idea with a preference for enclosed space and contact.
- **Houdini UV Layout**: raster search with a rotation step (none, 90, 180 or a custom number of divisions of 360
  degrees), rectangles, UDIM tiles or shapes as targets, scale "largest packable" or fixed, packing into cavities,
  packing around islands it does not move, and islands that do not fit lined up outside the target.
- **Blender** (Chris Blackbourn's draft): a conventional packer for the largest 1000 islands, the Alpaca shelves for
  the rest, a bitmap mode loosely based on xatlas for exact shapes.
- **UVPackmaster** (closed source; only its documented options): rotation step, a time-limited heuristic search,
  pixel margins, locked islands, groups, per-island texel density, CUDA or Vulkan. TABI's authors report it failing to
  finish on 4 % of their atlases, overlapping on 7.5 % and missing the gutter on over 90 % (their claim, not checked).
- **sparrow**: exploration and compression phases; colliding items moved one at a time to sampled positions refined
  by coordinate descent; overlap measured by inscribed circles and weighted by guided local search.

Sources: [xatlas source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jpcy/xatlas/master/source/xatlas/xatlas.cpp),
[Houdini UV Layout](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/nodes/sop/uvlayout.html),
[Blender packer draft](https://archive.blender.org/wiki/2024/wiki/User:Chris_Blackbourn/BlogDraft.html),
[Blender PR 105821](https://projects.blender.org/blender/blender/pulls/105821),
[TABI, arXiv 2602.07782](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07782v1),
[sparrow, arXiv 2509.13329](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13329v3),
[jagua-rs](https://www.github.com/JeroenGar/jagua-rs),
[GFPack++, arXiv 2406.07579](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07579v1),
[Burke et al., bottom-left fill](https://eprints.nottingham.ac.uk/52345/1/594772.pdf),
[UVPackmaster](https://glukoz.gumroad.com/l/uvpackmaster),
[UVPackmaster 4](https://digitalproduction.com/2026/01/23/uvpackmaster-4-unwraps-a-smarter-way-to-pack/).

| 2 | Requirement: a rotation constraint per island: any rotation, 180 degrees, 90 degrees, 45 degrees (and Upright: none). The maintainer: "have rotation constraint any rotation, 180 deg, 90deg, 45deg" |
| 3 | Requirement: packing into an assigned rectangle (the Target Area of UV Session 126). The maintainer: "can use assigned rect" |
| 4 | Requirement: packing with and without scaling the islands. The maintainer: "allow to scale and not" |
| 5 | Requirement: Blender's pinned UVs are read. Keep Pinned Islands and Target Area are settings of a set's run (UV decision 159) and never affect each other: with Keep Pinned Islands, every island with a pinned UV is left untouched; without a Target Area the others are packed around it; with a Target Area the area is packed as an empty sheet, with no obstacles, kept pinned islands included. The maintainer: "able to read blender's pinned uv"; "keep pinned island ticked means any pinned vertex will be untouched, regardless of there is target-area or not"; "target-area ticked we always pack to that area, regardless there are other island exist on there or not (including pinned island), there are no obstacles, the sheet on that area are empty" |
| 6 | Requirement: a priority per object, set with a slider: the higher it is, the closer the islands of that object are placed together; a lower one gives the packer more freedom to pack them densely. The maintainer: "able to prioritize based on object, perhaps some slider to adjust \"priority\" the higher priority, all island of the same object would be placed closer, less priority would means more freedom" |
| 7 | Requirement: an island can be straightened by marked edges: an edge marked Horizontal, Vertical or Either (either axis) is turned to lie along that axis; the rotation constraint then applies from that angle. The mark is set from Blender's Edge menu. With several marked edges on one island, the island takes the rotation that fits all of them best, each edge weighted by its UV length; an edge that stays off its axis by more than a tolerance is reported. The maintainer: "i also want to have straighten island by giving 2 vertice on island then we could constraint it always aligned horzontally, vertically or both"; "we could add menu mark uv-align-edge on blender edge menu?"; "ok i think best fit is better, add this to requirement" |

### Proposals (open)

- **Method:** a raster packer in C++ (pixel masks per allowed orientation, dilated by the margin so the gap is exact;
  the target area as the grid, pinned islands painted first; largest first, each where the layout grows least from
  the chosen corner, into holes first), with an optional time-limited improvement search (other orders, moves of
  single islands, several threads, the best layout kept), the useful part of UVPackmaster's heuristic search and of
  sparrow's local search. Recommended over no-fit polygons (slow, margins not in pixels), sparrow-style search alone
  (minutes per set) and learned packers (training, overlaps).
- **Scale (decision 4):** with scaling, the largest factor at which everything fits, found by halving the interval
  (6 to 10 packs, affordable in C++); this would revisit "straight to Min" (UV decision 156), which was chosen because
  packs were slow.
- **Priority (decision 6):** a candidate position also pays for its distance from the islands of the same object
  already placed, weighted by the object's priority; higher-priority objects placed first. No packer found documents
  exactly this; UVPackmaster's groups come closest.
- **Align edges (decision 7):** an edge attribute `fss_uv_align` (none, horizontal, vertical, either); entries in the
  3D view's Edge menu and context menu and in the UV editor's UV menu and context menu (Mark UV Align, Clear UV
  Align); an overlay drawing marked edges; Select UV Align Edges. Automatic straightening without marks as further
  modes: by a 3D axis, or by the island's tightest bounding box.
- **First step:** time the current packer's steps on the cockpit sets, as the baseline.
- **Until the C++ packer exists:** the Python fixes of the scattering (prefer the raster layout when islands are kept;
  the layout corner applied with kept islands) are on hold.

### Not Done

No code. The Target Area (UV Session 126) and the texture set blocks (UV Session 127) are built, tested and deployed as files, not committed.

### What Was Built

- `native/uv_pack/` (`fss_uv_pack.h`, `fss_uv_pack.cpp`, `build.cmd`, `README.md`): the DLL
  `fss_blender_tools/lib/uv_pack/windows-x64/fss_uv_pack.dll`, interface version 1, MSVC x64, `/O2 /fp:precise /MT

  `Shape`, `Grid` and the raster helpers are gone. The former module is kept as `tests/uv_fill_reference.py`, the
  reference for the comparison.
- Decision 13 in part: without the DLL, Pack, Pack All and Check All refuse with the reason (`uv_marks._packer_missing`).
  Blender's Pack Islands is still used; it goes with step (c).

## Session 5: Our Own Packer, Turns and Scale to Fill (Step c)

### Decisions

| No. | Decision |
| :--- | :--- |
| 19 | With Scale to Fill (decision 9), each zone, parent and object mark counts at its Min; the facing blend between Min and Max is not used. The maintainer chose this when asked which value of a mark with a range sets an island's ratio |
| 20 | A Free island is first straightened by its oriented bounding box (decision 10's 36 steps, refined); then the turns are preferred in this order: none, 180 degrees, 90 degrees, then 45 degrees. The maintainer: "i feels that no rotation is highest score, 180 second, 90 degrees 3rd highest score, 45, ..., but i think it depend on the island shape, perhaps we take the oriented bounding box of the island first, straighten it, then apply the scoring above"; and on the weight of the work: "most of the packing are done with only packing in 90 degrees or 180 degrees rotation allowance, its rare on free rotation" |

How this session applies decision 20: the order of preference is the last tie-break of a position, after how much the
layout grows, an enclosed space, the contact with neighbours and the distance from the origin; a straightened Free
island then turns as a Quarter one (not in 45-degree steps).

### What Was Built

- `fss_uv_pack` interface version 2: a turn set per island (Upright, Flip, Quarter, Eighth) and a base angle in
  place of the quarter turn of version 1; `fss_uv_straighten` (36 steps of 10 degrees, then 1 and 0.1 degree, the
  smallest turn in -45 to 45 degrees, none when the box does not shrink).
- `uv_fill`: `place` and `repair` take the turn set and the base angle; `apply` turns by any angle (exactly for
  multiples of 90 degrees); `straighten`; `place` tries the islands in three orders (`ORDERS`: `BOX_POWER` 0.8, 0.6
  and 0.4) and keeps the tightest layout.
- `uv_pack`: Blender's Pack Islands is gone, with its temporary mesh, the proxy islands, the CONCAVE and AABB rounds
  and the comparison of two layouts (`TextureSet.fill`). `TextureSet.pack` scales the islands and places them with
  `uv_fill.place`; without pinned islands the layout is slid back to half the gap from the origin, which the raster
  rounds up to a cell. Should the raster hold no layout, the islands stay on the shelves of the preparation (counted
  and reported; never seen on the cockpit). The direction marks map to turn sets; Flip now turns by 180 degrees
  (before, it was held as Upright).
- Scale to Fill (decisions 9 and 19): a tick per texture set (`fss_uv_scale_fill`, off by default) between Keep
  Pinned Islands and Target Area. The densities are the ratios (marks at their Min, material factor, visibility,
  multipliers set by hand) times one scale; the search starts at 1, aims from each k just inside, keeps the bracket of
  the largest scale that fitted and the smallest that did not, and stops within 0.2 % or after 10 packs. Mode
  `scaled`, with the scale and the target density reached in the report, the tooltip and the row ("scaled x1.20, fits
  ..."); the Check Results room is "full".
- The Eighth direction mark (45-degree steps).
- `fss_blender_tools` 0.98.0.

## Session 9: Crash of the UV Align Overlay When Moving UVs

- **Date:** 20261116
- **Developer:** uraymeiviar
- **Agent:** Claude Code (Opus 5.5)

The maintainer reported that Blender crashed when grabbing and moving UVs in the UV editor on a mesh with UV Align
marks (E2 cockpit, after several marks and Edit Mode toggles).

### Cause

The crash log's stack: `_drop_cache` (the depsgraph handler of `uv_align_draw`) clearing the cache, then
`bpy_bmesh_dealloc`, `BM_data_layer_free`, and an access violation in the heap's free. The cache of Session 8 held the
BMesh wrapper so that its cached edges stayed valid. A wrapper freed after Blender has freed or rebuilt the edit mesh
(leaving and entering Edit Mode, an undo) still removes its Python pointer layer from that mesh: a use after free.
Moving UVs fires a depsgraph update on every step, which cleared the cache and freed the old wrapper.

### Fix

- `uv_align_draw.marked(me, bm)` caches only the edge count and `(edge index, kind)` pairs per mesh; each draw takes
  the BMesh fresh (`bmesh.from_edit_mesh`), and the cache is rebuilt when the edge count differs or after a depsgraph
  update. No BMesh data lives longer than one draw.
- `fss_blender_tools` 0.101.1.

### Verified

- `test_uv_pack` passes, all tests. `test_uv_align_overlay` now also runs three rounds of a UV draw, Edit Mode left
  and entered, every UV moved, a depsgraph update, and checks that the cache holds only integers.
- The old pattern (a BMesh wrapper kept across Edit Mode toggles and freed in the depsgraph handler) did not crash in
  a background Blender: the freed memory is not reused there, so no background test reproduces the crash.

Not verified: the fix in a Blender with a window, by moving UVs with marked edges; the maintainer's check.

## Session 10: Second Crash of the UV Align Overlay; No BMesh While Drawing

- **Date:** 20261116
- **Developer:** uraymeiviar
- **Agent:** Claude Code (Opus 5.5)

The maintainer reported that Blender (0.101.1) still crashed when moving UVs, now on the E2 cockpit with every object
in Edit Mode.

### Cause

The new crash log's stack: the overlay's draw callback, `BPy_BMElem_CreatePyObject`, `BM_data_layer_add`,
`update_data_blocks`, an access violation in `BLI_mempool_free`. Blender keeps the Python object of each BMesh
element in a layer of the edit mesh, added when the first Python element is made and removed when the Python BMesh is
freed. Adding or removing it moves the data of every element of the mesh. Doing so while Blender draws, or while a
modal operator holds pointers into that data (a UV grab), frees memory still in use. The fix of Session 9 (no BMesh kept
between draws) did not touch this: each draw still made Python elements.

### Fix

- `uv_align_draw` never touches the BMesh while drawing. `refresh()` copies the marked edges into numpy arrays (3D
  ends, UV ends of the face sides, kind, face selection) through `BMesh.to_mesh` into a scratch mesh: it makes no
  Python elements, so the edit mesh is not changed. Meshes without the attribute (known in Edit Mode from
  `Mesh.attributes`) are skipped.
- The copy runs in a timer, between events, and waits (0.2 s steps) while a modal operator of an editing kind
  (`TRANSFORM_OT`, `MESH_OT`, `UV_OT`, `IMAGE_OT`, `OBJECT_OT`, `SCULPT_OT`, `PAINT_OT`) runs. A depsgraph update of a
  mesh or an object drops the copies and schedules the timer; a draw that finds a mesh without a copy schedules it too.
  Until the copy is made, nothing is drawn for that mesh: during a UV grab the overlay is away, and it comes back on
  release.
- The scratch mesh `.fss_uv_align_overlay` is kept and reused (no users, so not saved; removed at unregister): adding
  and removing a mesh on every copy rebuilds the depsgraph relations, which reports the objects as updated and would
  drop the copies again without end.
- `fss_blender_tools` 0.101.2.

### Verified

- `test_uv_align_overlay`: the copies after each depsgraph update; a depsgraph update after a copy keeps it (no loop);
  the 3D and UV lines as before; a selection change drops the copy; three rounds of Edit Mode left and entered with the
  UVs moved, the copies following the moved UVs (0.03 in x); the cache holds only numpy arrays.
- Probes: `obj.to_mesh()` in Edit Mode returns the mesh as it was on entering Edit Mode, and `update_from_editmode`
  tags the mesh, so neither serves.

Not verified: the timer and the drawing in a Blender with a window (the background runs no timers); that a UV grab no
longer crashes. The maintainer's check.
